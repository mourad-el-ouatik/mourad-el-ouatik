## 🎯 About Me

<img align="right" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/devSouvik/devSouvik/master/gif3.gif" width="350" alt="Coding" />

I'm a **5th-year Cybersecurity Engineering student** at ENSA Marrakech, specializing in **Cyber Defense & Embedded Telecommunications**. I'm passionate about building resilient security solutions at the intersection of **Artificial Intelligence** and **Cybersecurity**.

🔐 **What I do:**
- Design **Zero Trust Architectures** (NIST SP 800-207 compliant)
- Build **AI-driven SOC** pipelines with LLMs and RAG
- Automate security with **DevSecOps** practices
- Research **Post-Quantum Cryptography** and **AI-powered threat detection**

💡 **Mission:** Leverage cutting-edge technology to create measurable security impact — reducing MTTR by **85%**, false positives by **40%**, and deployment times by **60%**.

⚡ **Fun fact:** I co-founded the first MUN club at ENSA and led as President of Self-Dev Club!
